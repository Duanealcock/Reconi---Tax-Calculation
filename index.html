      How this is calculated

      Retirement deduction. Contributions are deductible up to 27.5% of gross income, capped at R430,000 a year. Amounts above the cap carry forward to future years.

      Rebates. A primary rebate of R17,820 applies to everyone; a secondary rebate of R9,765 is added from age 65; a tertiary rebate of R3,249 is added from age 75.

      Medical scheme fees tax credit (MSFTC). A fixed monthly credit: R376 for each of the first two members covered, and R254 for each dependant after that.

      Additional medical expenses credit (AMTC). For taxpayers 65+, or with a qualifying disability: 33.3% of (medical aid contributions over 3× the annual MSFTC, plus other qualifying expenses). Under 65 without a disability: 25% of that same total, less 7.5% of taxable income.

      UIF. 1% of monthly remuneration is contributed by both employee and employer, capped at a monthly remuneration ceiling of R17,712 (max R177.12 each, per month).

      Donations (s18A). Donations to approved public benefit organisations are deductible up to 10% of taxable income, calculated before the donations deduction itself. Amounts above the cap carry forward to future years.

      Covers salaried income only. Excludes travel and other allowances, fringe benefits, capital gains, other tax credits, and provisional tax for non-salaried income. Anything ticked under “Other items on the standard return” needs its own schedule and isn’t reflected in the figures above.

        How this is calculated

        Standard rate. Companies pay a flat 27% on taxable income for years of assessment ending in the 2026/2027 tax year.

        Small Business Corporation (SBC). A qualifying SBC is taxed on a graduated scale: 0% up to R99,000, 7% up to R365,000, 21% up to R550,000 plus R18,620, and 27% above that plus R57,470. Qualifying broadly requires all shareholders to be natural persons with no other company interests, gross income under R20 million, and investment/personal-service income under 20% of total receipts — confirm eligibility before relying on this.

        Not included. Dividends tax (20% withheld when after-tax profit is distributed to shareholders), capital gains, provisional tax payment dates, and any deferred tax adjustments.

        Calculation checklist

          Rate applied

          Flat trust rate: 45% on income retained in the trust. Trusts get no rebates and no tax-free threshold.
          How this is calculated

          Conduit principle. This estimates tax on income retained in the trust. Income vested in (distributed to) beneficiaries during the year is taxed in their hands, at their own rates, not at the trust's flat rate.

          Special trusts. A trust set up solely for a beneficiary with a disability, or for minor beneficiaries of a deceased estate, is instead taxed on the same sliding scale as an individual — use the Individual Tax tab for that calculation.

          Not included. Capital gains (trusts include 80% of gains in taxable income, versus 40% for individuals), donations tax, and distributions to non-resident beneficiaries.
